Renewing the guardian visa each year follows a similar pattern to other Non-O categories, with its own specific timing and bank balance rules. Here's the complete process.

## When to start your guardian visa renewal

Thai immigration allows renewal applications up to **30 days before** your current permission expires. We recommend starting the process around **three months ahead** to leave plenty of time for any document issues.

## Documents required for guardian visa renewal Thailand

- Current passport with your existing visa
- Updated school enrolment letter confirming your child remains enrolled
- Bank evidence showing the continuous 500,000 THB balance
- Updated health insurance certificate
- Completed renewal application form

## Step-by-step guardian visa renewal process

1. Confirm your child's continued school enrolment with an updated letter
2. Check your bank balance has the required 3-month continuous history
3. Renew your health insurance if it's due to lapse
4. Submit your complete application at Chiang Mai immigration
5. Receive your renewed extension stamp

## 90-day reporting: don't forget this

Separate from your annual renewal, guardian visa holders must report their current address to Thai immigration **every 90 days** if remaining in Thailand continuously. The penalty for non-compliance is **2,000 THB**.

## Common reasons guardian visa renewals are refused

An outdated or unclear school enrolment letter, insufficient bank balance seasoning, and lapsed health insurance are the most common issues we see at renewal time.

## Renewal countdown: what to do and when

Around three months out: confirm school enrolment status. Around two months out: check your bank balance seasoning is on track. Within 30 days: submit your application.

## Bank balance: how it must look over a renewal year

Your 500,000 THB balance needs **at least 3 months of continuous history** before your renewal application: a single dip below the threshold during that period can reset the seasoning clock.

## Guardian visa renewal: step-by-step at Chiang Mai immigration

Submissions are processed at the Chiang Mai Immigration Office, Promenada Resort Mall, 2nd Floor, Monday–Friday, **8:30am–4:30pm**.

## How far in advance can you apply?

Up to 30 days before expiry, per Thai immigration rules, and applying right at the edge of that window leaves no room for correcting any document issues, so earlier is safer.

## What to do if your child changes schools mid-year

Contact us as soon as the change happens. Your guardian visa basis depends on continued enrolment at a recognised school, so a mid-year change needs to be reflected in your documentation before your next renewal.

## Frequently asked questions

**What's the penalty for overstaying if I miss my renewal?** 500 THB per day if your permission expires without a completed extension: apply within the 30-day window to avoid this.
