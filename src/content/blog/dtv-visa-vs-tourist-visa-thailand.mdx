For remote workers weighing how to stay in Thailand long-term, the choice often comes down to the DTV or repeated tourist visa cycles. Here's the real comparison, including the cost math.

## Key takeaways

The DTV costs more upfront but eliminates the recurring cost and hassle of visa runs. The break-even point depends on how long you plan to stay and how often you'd otherwise need to exit and re-enter.

## What is the DTV visa — and what does "Destination Thailand Visa" actually mean?

A five-year visa with 180 days of stay per entry, built for remote workers, freelancers, and soft-power applicants, costing approximately **USD 280**.

## Tourist visa Thailand 2026 — what you're actually working with

A standard tourist entry grants **60 days, extendable once to 90 days** — after which you need to exit the country and re-enter (a "visa run") to start a fresh period.

## DTV vs tourist visa: side-by-side comparison

| | DTV | Tourist Visa |
|---|---|---|
| Stay per entry | 180 days (extendable to 360) | 60–90 days |
| Validity | 5 years | Single entry, repeated applications |
| Cost | ~USD 280 | Lower per-application, but repeats |
| Remote work | Explicitly permitted | Grey area, not designed for this |

## The real cost of staying on tourist visas: the visa run maths

For someone staying 8 months a year on tourist visas, each border run costs **USD 150–400** in flights, transport, and time. After roughly **two border runs**, the DTV becomes the more cost-effective option — and that's before counting the time and disruption of each trip.

## Who qualifies for the DTV visa? The five pathways

Remote employment, self-employment/freelance work, Thai soft power (cultural programs like Muay Thai or cooking courses), medical treatment, and dependent status (family members of a primary DTV holder).

## The 500,000 THB requirement: the biggest barrier and how to meet it

Applicants need **500,000 THB (~USD 14,500)** in liquid savings, held for **90 consecutive days** before applying. This is the requirement that trips up the most prospective applicants — start seasoning the funds well before you intend to apply.

## When the tourist visa is the right call

For short trips — a few weeks to a couple of months — the tourist visa remains simpler and avoids the DTV's documentation requirements entirely. The DTV only makes sense once you're planning a genuinely extended stay.
