How much does a guardian visa actually cost? Here's the full breakdown: government fees, the bank requirement, and the realistic total to budget for.

## The government fee: 1,900 THB

The annual immigration extension fee is **1,900 THB**, the core, recurring government cost of holding the guardian visa.

## The bank requirement

A **500,000 THB** deposit is required in a Thai bank account (roughly USD 13,700–14,400). As with other Thai visa categories, this is a deposit you retain access to, not a fee you pay. The real cost is the opportunity cost of holding those funds.

## School documentation costs

Obtaining your child's enrolment letter and supporting school documentation typically costs **0–2,000 THB**, depending on the school's own administrative fees.

## Bank letter fee

Your Thai bank will charge approximately **500 THB** for the official letter confirming your deposit balance, required as part of your application.

## Visa agent fees

If you use an agent for document preparation and submission, budget **3,000–8,000 THB**.

## Summary: guardian visa Thailand cost breakdown

| Item | Approximate cost |
|---|---|
| Government extension fee | 1,900 THB |
| Bank letter | ~500 THB |
| School documentation | 0–2,000 THB |
| Health insurance (annual) | 15,000–40,000 THB |

| Agency support (optional) | 3,000–8,000 THB |

## Planning tip: open your Thai bank account early

The 500,000 THB deposit needs to season for **2 months (60 days)** before your initial application, or **3 months (90 days)** before annual renewals. Open the account and fund it well ahead of your intended application date.

## Guardian visa cost: full itemised breakdown

Adding government fees, school documentation, bank letter, insurance, and optional agency support together, total annual outlay typically runs **25,000–55,000 THB per year**, not counting the standing 500,000 THB deposit, which remains yours throughout.

## Can you do it yourself (without an agent)?

Yes, the process doesn't require an agent. Many parents manage it independently, particularly for straightforward renewals once the initial application has been completed successfully.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is the 500,000 THB a one-time cost?** No, it's a standing deposit you maintain (with the required seasoning) for as long as you hold the visa, not a one-time fee. See our [full guardian visa requirements](/guardian-visa-thailand-requirements/) for the complete documentation picture.

**Does the cost change between initial application and renewal?** The core fees stay the same, but renewal requires longer bank seasoning (3 months vs. 2 months initially). See our [renewal guide](/guardian-visa-renewal-thailand/) for the full process.
