Thai language study is the most popular ED visa route in Chiang Mai. Here's exactly how it works, what it costs, and what's expected of you.

## What is the Thai language ED visa?

A Non-Immigrant education visa obtained through enrolment at a Ministry of Education-registered Thai language school, beginning as a 90-day stamp with extensions available every 90 days, typically for one year per enrolment.

## Why Chiang Mai for Thai language study?

Chiang Mai offers clear, standard Thai dialect instruction, a lower cost of living than Bangkok, immigration staff experienced with the ED visa process, competitive tuition rates, and an established expat community to support the transition.

## How much Thai do you have to study?

Classes typically run **3–10 hours per week**, with **80% or more attendance** required over each extension period.

## How to get a Thai language ED visa in Chiang Mai

Enrol at a licensed school, apply for the visa from abroad (generally the cheapest route), then extend every 90 days at Promenada Resort Mall immigration office with the required documentation from your school.

## Cost of a Thai language ED visa in Chiang Mai

Approximately **20,000–38,000 THB per year**, including tuition and extension fees combined.

## Can you work while on a Thai language ED visa?

No — the ED visa doesn't permit employment. Remote work for foreign clients or employers exists in something of a grey area; if this matters to your situation, a DTV may be a more appropriate fit than an ED visa built around study.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is applying from outside Thailand actually cheaper?** Yes — converting from inside Thailand typically adds 10,000–15,000 THB in conversion fees compared to applying from abroad first.

**What happens if I don't hit 80% attendance?** This puts your renewal at risk — schools report attendance to immigration, so consistent attendance matters for maintaining your visa status, not just for learning the language.
