Every document the Thai retirement visa (Non-Immigrant O-A) requires, for both your initial application and annual renewal, organised so nothing catches you out at submission.

## Eligibility criteria at a glance

You must be at least 50 years old, have no criminal convictions, meet one of the financial thresholds (800,000 THB in a Thai bank account, or 65,000 THB monthly income), and carry qualifying health insurance.

## Initial Non-O-A application documents

### Passport documentation

Valid for at least 18 months from your application date, with sufficient blank visa pages.

### Visa application form

The completed TM.87 form.

### Photographs

Four recent passport-style photographs, 4×6 cm.

### Bank evidence (800,000 THB route)

A bank letter confirming the 800,000 THB balance, with the deposit seasoned for at least 2 months before your initial application, plus certified copies of your bank passbook.

### Income documentation (65,000 THB monthly route)

An embassy-issued income letter confirming your monthly income meets the threshold, if you're using this route instead of the bank deposit.

### Combination route documentation

If combining income and savings, both the bank letter and income documentation are required together.

### Health insurance certificate

Coverage meeting Thailand's minimum thresholds: 40,000 THB outpatient and 400,000 THB inpatient.

### Medical certificate

Issued within the last 6 months, signed by a Thai-licensed doctor.

### Criminal record check

A police clearance certificate from your home country (or country of recent residence).

### Application fee

2,000–3,000 THB, paid at the time of application.

## Annual renewal document checklist

### Extension form and passport

The TM.7 form, plus your current passport.

### Photographs

Two recent passport photos.

### Bank evidence

Continuous evidence of an 800,000+ THB balance, seasoned for 3 months for renewal applications (longer than the initial 2-month requirement).

### Updated income documentation

If using the income route, updated income letters and statements covering the renewal period.

### Health insurance proof

Active policy documentation, confirming coverage remains in force and meets the minimum thresholds.

### Thai bank letter

May be requested in addition to your passbook evidence, depending on the immigration office.

### TM.30 document

Address notification confirming your current registered residence.

### Fee

1,900 THB for the annual extension.

## Approved health insurance providers in Thailand

Commonly accepted Thai insurers include Thai Life Insurance, Bangkok Insurance, AXA Thailand, Allianz Ayudhya, Thai Panasonic Insurance, and Viriyah Insurance. Annual premiums for Thai-issued policies typically range **12,000–25,000 THB**, varying by age and coverage level.

## Common rejection reasons and how to avoid them

- **Insufficient bank seasoning**: under 2 months for initial applications, under 3 months for renewals
- **Balance discrepancies** between the bank letter and your passbook
- **Expired or missing insurance** coverage at the time of application
- **Outdated medical certificates**: these need to be recent, not gathered weeks in advance
- **Incorrect income letter sources**: the letter needs to come from the right issuing authority
- **Substandard photos** that don't meet the size/background specification

## Timeline for initial application

Allow **4–6 weeks** from starting document collection to approval, particularly if gathering an overseas police check or legalising documents.

## Timeline for annual renewal

Renewals move faster, typically **1–2 weeks**, since most of your documentation simply needs updating rather than gathering from scratch.

## Final checklist

Before submitting: confirm bank seasoning meets the period required for your application type, your insurance is active and meets minimum coverage, your medical certificate is recent, and every figure matches exactly across all documents.
