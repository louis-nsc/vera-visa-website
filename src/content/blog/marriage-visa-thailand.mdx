A complete overview of the Thailand marriage visa — what it requires, what it costs, and what happens if your circumstances change.

## What is the Thailand marriage visa?

Formally a Non-Immigrant O visa granted on the basis of marriage to a Thai national. It allows one-year renewable stays, with no age restriction — unlike the retirement visa, which requires applicants to be 50 or older.

## Financial requirements — lower than you might expect

- **Bank deposit:** 400,000 THB (compared to 800,000 THB for the retirement visa)
- **Income method:** 40,000 THB monthly

Funds must be held in a Thai bank account for at least **two months** before your extension application.

## Documents you'll need

- Valid passport with at least six months remaining validity
- Thai marriage certificate (Kor Ror 2 and Kor Ror 3), or a registered overseas marriage with the required legalisation
- Joint photographs, indoors and outdoors at your home, showing genuine cohabitation
- Proof of residence — rental agreement or property ownership documentation
- Thai spouse's national ID and house registration

## Step-by-step application process

1. Obtain a Non-Immigrant O visa from a Thai embassy
2. Apply for a one-year extension at your local immigration office
3. Renew annually with updated financial documentation

## 90-day reporting and re-entry permits

90-day address reporting is mandatory if you remain in Thailand continuously. A re-entry permit is required before any departure, or your visa is cancelled on exit.

## If your relationship ends: divorce and visa status

The marriage visa becomes invalid upon divorce. If this happens, you'll need to transition to a different visa category — contact us as early as possible to plan the right next step.

## Frequently asked questions

**Are same-sex couples eligible?** Yes — same-sex couples became eligible for the marriage visa following Thailand's Marriage Equality Act, which took effect in January 2025.
