Working legally in Thailand as a foreigner requires two separate approvals working together. Here's the complete picture, from the visa through to the permit itself.

## Key takeaways

The Non-Immigrant B visa and the work permit are two separate documents. You need both to work legally in Thailand, and neither substitutes for the other.

## Non-Immigrant B visa vs. work permit: two different things

The Non-B visa is your immigration status; the work permit is your specific authorisation to perform a defined job for a defined employer. Holding one without the other doesn't authorise you to work.

## Who needs a Non-Immigrant B visa and work permit?

Any foreigner performing work in Thailand, whether employed by a Thai company or otherwise engaged in local work activities, needs both. There's no general exemption for short engagements.

## Employer requirements: what your company must prove

Sponsoring companies must maintain a minimum of **2,000,000 THB** in paid-up registered capital for each foreign work permit holder, along with proper registration and compliance documentation.

## Non-Immigrant B visa: documents required

Passport, completed application form, employment evidence from your Thai employer, and company registration documents form the core of a Non-B application.

## Work permit: documents required

Your personal documents (passport, qualifications, medical certificate) alongside your employer's registration, capital evidence, and payroll documentation.

## The e-Work Permit system

Thailand transitioned to a mandatory online work permit system, streamlining what was previously an entirely paper-based process. See our [e-Work Permit guide](/thailand-e-work-permit-2026/) for the full portal walkthrough.

## Restricted occupations: what foreigners cannot do

Thai law reserves a defined list of professions for Thai nationals only. We confirm whether your intended role is open to foreign workers before any application begins.

## 90-day reporting and visa extension

Once working in Thailand, you'll need to file 90-day reports if remaining continuously, and renew both your Non-B visa and work permit annually, in coordination with each other.

## Visa and work permit fees

**Non-B visa fees:**
- Single-entry (90-day stay): 2,000 THB
- Multiple-entry (one year validity): 5,000 THB

**Work permit fees:**
- 3 months: 750 THB
- 6 months: 1,500 THB
- One year: 3,000 THB

## Processing timeline

Work permits typically take **7–10 business days** once a complete application is submitted.
